Instagram exchange

Two matched people, side by side. Each can tap Instagram, type their handle and mark themselves ready. Nobody sees a handle until both are ready. Then both handles are shown and the match closes.

Rules in this demo

  1. Either person can start. Their handle stays hidden from the other person while they wait.
  2. The other person only sees "Quiet Fox is ready to swap Instagrams".
  3. Until both are ready, either person can change their handle or take it back.
  4. When both are ready, each sees the other's handle, the chat becomes read-only and the match is marked closed.
  5. Handles must be 1 to 30 letters, numbers, periods or underscores, as on Instagram.
